Affiliate disclosure

Some outbound booking links on RouteRiff are affiliate links. If you click one of those links and later make an eligible purchase, RouteRiff may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

How this affects the product

Affiliate economics do not change the route-sanity score, distance estimate, route warnings or route optimization logic. Booking providers are presented as optional exits from the planning workflow.

RouteRiff may route an affiliate click through a first-party redirect endpoint so it can count the click before sending you to the independent provider. The redirect destination is selected from a fixed list of approved partner links.

Compare before you pay

Prices and availability change frequently. Compare the final price, taxes, cancellation rules, inclusions and support terms on the provider's site before completing a purchase.
