Privacy

RouteRiff is an experimental browser-first travel planning tool.

What the MVP processes

When you build a route, place names are geocoded so they can be shown on a map. The browser may store recent routes, coordinates and a geocoding cache locally on your device so a route can reopen without repeating every place lookup. Shared trip URLs may contain stop names and coordinates so the recipient can see the shared route without re-geocoding it. Do not paste passport numbers, payment details, booking references or other sensitive information.

Product analytics

The MVP records coarse product events such as page view, trip built, play, share, remix and affiliate click. Event metadata is intentionally limited to non-sensitive counters or source labels; the analytics event does not include your itinerary text.

Third parties

Map tiles and geocoding rely on OpenStreetMap-related services. Booking buttons open independent travel providers. Those providers process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes

This MVP will evolve. If accounts, cloud-saved trips or media uploads are introduced, this notice should be updated before those features are enabled.
