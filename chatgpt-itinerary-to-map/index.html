Best input formats

Day 1: Tokyo Day 2: Hakone Day 3: Kyoto Day 4: Osaka

Bullets, day labels, arrows and one-place-per-line inputs work best. TripRemix visualizes what you paste; it does not silently invent replacement stops.

What the check adds

  • See every main stop on one map.
  • Spot long jumps or obvious backtracking.
  • Estimate the rough travel load.
  • Optimize the middle-stop order as a simple geographic sanity check.
  • Play the route, share it and let someone else remix it.

Still verify the real trip

AI plans and map estimates can both be wrong. Confirm exact roads, trains, flights, opening hours, visas, closures and booking rules with official sources before paying or travelling.
