What RouteRiff does not claim

The current checker uses geographic distance and simple transport heuristics. It does not know your exact train timetable, road conditions, border rules or flight schedule. Treat it as a route-shape check, then verify the real transport with official operators.

Good inputs

Paste the overnight cities or major stops from an AI itinerary, spreadsheet, blog or notes. Day labels, bullets, arrows and one-place-per-line formats work best.
