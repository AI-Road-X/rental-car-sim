Route order first, transport mode second

Do not choose a train or flight for a leg that may disappear after you reorder the cities. First make sure the sequence is sensible. Then compare the actual schedule, station or airport, door-to-door time, fare and cancellation rules.

Why this matters in China

China is large enough that a single misplaced city can turn into a major transfer day. The hidden cost is not only the scheduled travel time: hotel checkout, station or airport access, waiting, arrival transport and check-in all reduce usable sightseeing time.

What RouteRiff checks

  • the geographic shape of the route;
  • long jumps;
  • obvious backtracking;
  • the largest detour stop;
  • rough transfer load;
  • whether a simpler order reduces straight-line distance.

What RouteRiff does not replace

It is not a live China rail or flight engine. It does not verify current high-speed rail schedules, station choices, flight availability, visa rules, closures, prices or ticket inventory. Verify those details with official sources and booking providers before paying.
