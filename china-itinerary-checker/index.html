Useful for AI-generated China routes

If ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or a blog gives you a multi-city plan, paste only the major overnight cities first. RouteRiff can flag long jumps, likely detours and rough transfer load, then create a correction prompt you can send back to the same AI.

What RouteRiff does not replace

It is not a live China rail or flight engine. Verify current high-speed rail schedules, station choices, flight availability, entry rules, closures and prices with official sources and booking providers.

A simple workflow

  1. Draft the itinerary.
  2. Check the city order on RouteRiff.
  3. Fix backtracking or excessive transfer load.
  4. Send the corrected route back to AI.
  5. Verify real transport and book.
