Privacy

The itinerary is processed in your browser. Place names are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service for geocoding; map tiles load from OpenStreetMap. Your last route and geocoding cache may be stored locally in your browser. Do not enter passport, payment, booking-reference or other sensitive data.

Travel & affiliate terms

Distances, time estimates, transport modes and route scores are directional estimates, not live travel advice. Verify schedules, visas, weather, closures and supplier terms with official sources. Booking links lead to independent third parties; their prices, refunds, support and policies apply.
