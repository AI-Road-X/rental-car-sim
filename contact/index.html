CONTACT & FEEDBACK

Contact RouteRiff

RouteRiff is an experimental route-checking product. The public project issue tracker is the current contact channel for reporting bugs, accessibility problems, confusing route results, privacy questions and product feedback.

Open a RouteRiff issue →

What to include

For a route audit problem, describe the number and type of stops, the result you expected, and what happened instead. You can include a minimal example with public place names or approximate coordinates. Do not post private travel plans, booking references, email addresses, phone numbers or other personal information. GitHub issues are public and may be visible to anyone.

Privacy and booking links

RouteRiff processes pasted itinerary text in your browser. Its analytics record coarse product events rather than the itinerary text. Some optional booking links may earn a commission; that does not change the route score. For privacy or affiliate questions, use the same issue tracker and clearly label the topic so it can be routed appropriately.

What RouteRiff can help with

RouteRiff checks route shape, approximate geographic distance, likely backtracking and rough transfer load. It does not provide live transport times, prices, ticket availability or booking support. Contact the relevant transport operator or booking provider for those details.
