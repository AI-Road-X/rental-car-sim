What RouteRiff checks

  • long jumps between cities;
  • obvious backtracking and detour stops;
  • rough travel load across the trip;
  • overly travel-heavy pacing when day labels are present;
  • whether a simpler stop order reduces straight-line travel.

What it does not check yet

RouteRiff is not a live Japan rail planner. It does not currently calculate exact Shinkansen times, JR Pass value, luggage-forwarding schedules, attraction opening hours, weather or current ticket inventory. Verify those with official sources and booking providers.

Best input

Paste the overnight cities or major transfer stops from ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, a spreadsheet or your own notes. If your AI answer is long, RouteRiff can copy a cleanup prompt that asks the model to return only the major stops.
