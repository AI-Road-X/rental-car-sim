AI ITINERARY CHECKER

Sanity-check an AI travel itinerary before you book it.

Chatbots can produce convincing travel plans that still zig-zag across a city, stack too much transit into one day, or hide a long jump between attractive place names. RouteRiff is a second-pass checker: bring the main stops you already have, inspect them geographically, then copy a correction prompt back into the AI you prefer.

The second-pass workflow
1. Generate or collect a route
2. Paste the main stops
3. See the geography
4. Reorder or optimize
5. Copy the AI fix prompt
6. Verify live details

What RouteRiff deliberately does not guess

Live train times, road closures, visas, ticket inventory, opening hours and current prices should come from official or booking sources. RouteRiff focuses on the part that is easy to miss in text: the geographic shape and rough travel burden of the plan.
